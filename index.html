Keebo's golden rules
  1. Sit up straight. Feet flat. Wrists float, do not rest.
  2. Find the bumps on F and J with your index fingers.
  3. Each finger has its own keys. Go back to the home row after each key.
  4. Eyes on the screen. Do not look at your hands.
  5. Accuracy first. Speed comes by itself.
  6. 10–15 minutes every day beats 2 hours once a week.
